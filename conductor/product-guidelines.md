# Product Guidelines

## Tone and Voice
- **Accessible but Technically Precise:** The product should speak to a sophisticated audience of power users and developers. 
- **Professional:** Communication should be clear, concise, and focused on accuracy.
- **Objective:** When presenting model reviews and rankings, the UI should remain neutral and highlight the data provided by the LLMs.

## Visual Identity and UI (Existing)
- **Maintain Current Aesthetic:** Adhere to the existing design patterns established in the React frontend.
- **Focus on Clarity:** Ensure that the multi-stage process (Stage 1, 2, and 3) is clearly represented and easy for the user to follow.
- **Side-by-Side Comparison:** Prioritize the "tab view" or side-by-side comparison features that allow users to inspect individual model responses efficiently.

## Brand Messaging
- **The Council of Experts:** Position the tool as a way to consult a panel of expert AI models rather than just a single source.
- **Truth through Synthesis:** Emphasize that the highest quality answers come from the review and compilation of multiple perspectives.
